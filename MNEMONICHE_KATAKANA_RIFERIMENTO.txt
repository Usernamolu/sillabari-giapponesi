VOCALI (ア行)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

【ア】 - A
Visual: An Arrow pointing right.
Story: See the Arrow pointing down and right? "A" for Arrow.

【イ】 - I
Visual: An Eagle.
Story: Looks like an eagle looking down. "I" (Ea-gle, sounds like I) for Eagle.

【ウ】 - U
Visual: A U-turn.
Story: It looks like the path of someone doing a U-turn.

【エ】 - E
Visual: An Elevator.
Story: It looks like the doors of an elevator shutting. 

【オ】 - O
Visual: An Opera singer.
Story: Someone singing opera with their hands up screaming "Ohhhhh!".

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
RIGA K (カ行)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

【カ】 - KA
Visual: Someone cutting a stick.
Story: This is identical to the hiragana か, just with sharper, more blocky lines.

【キ】 - KI
Visual: A Key.
Story: Like the hiragana き it also looks like a key, just without the bottom loop.

【ク】 - KU
Visual: A Cook's hat.
Story: It looks like a long cook's hat. "Ku" for Cook.

【ケ】 - KE
Visual: A KEntucky fried chicken piece.
Story: It resembles the letter "K" for Kentucky.

【コ】 - KO
Visual: Two Corners.
Story: You can vividly see two corners, making a "Ko" sound.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
RIGA S (サ行)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

【サ】 - SA
Visual: Three SARdines.
Story: Looks like three sardines impaled on a skewer.

【シ】 - SHI
Visual: A SHIp.
Story: It looks like a smiley face looking up, or water splashing on a ship. (Careful not to confuse with Tsu ツ)

【ス】 - SU
Visual: A SUperman insignia.
Story: It looks like the diamond holding the Superman 'S'.

【セ】 - SE
Visual: SEe the teeth?
Story: Very similar to hiragana せ, just missing a piece!

【ソ】 - SO
Visual: One SOft serve ice cream.
Story: Imagine the top point dropping down to make the big stroke of soft serve.
